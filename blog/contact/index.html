Contact OctoProbe News

Updated Oct 17, 2026

The OctoProbe News editorial desk reads every message. The newsroom inbox is separate from the OctoProbe platform’s sales and support channels.

Press and Media Inquiries

press@octoprobe.ai. Include your outlet, deadline and the story you are asking about.

Tips

Send tips to press@octoprobe.ai with “Tip” in the subject line. Tell us what you know, how you know it and where records can be found. We protect sources who ask for confidentiality.

A note on security: email is not end-to-end encrypted. Do not send sensitive documents or anything that could identify you until we have agreed on a safer way to communicate.

Corrections

Email press@octoprobe.ai with “Correction” in the subject line. Our corrections policy explains what happens next.

OctoProbe Platform

Questions about the OctoProbe investigation platform go to team@octoprobe.ai, not the newsroom.
