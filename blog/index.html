FAQ

What is an OSINT investigation platform?
An OSINT investigation platform is a workspace that turns public clues—emails, phones, handles, names, domains, or photos—into a sourced investigation graph using premium APIs, public records, and related intel layers. OctoProbe AI is an OSINT investigation platform from Redwing Technologies with evidence on every link.
How does an identifier graph work?
An identifier graph maps entities and identifiers connected by evidenced edges. Each edge carries a source, timestamp, and confidence so analysts can explain why two nodes are linked. OctoProbe encodes the seed → expand → link → collect loop on a live graph.
What identifiers can OctoProbe start from?
Any clue works: an email, phone number, handle, name, domain, or photo. Use one, or combine several that already co-occur in public view.
Is OctoProbe a consumer reporting agency?
No. OctoProbe is not a consumer reporting agency. Results may not be used for FCRA-covered eligibility decisions such as credit, employment, insurance, or tenant screening.
Who is OctoProbe for?
Law firms, licensed private investigators, OSINT analysts, and opposition research or corporate intelligence teams who need sourced digital-footprint maps from public OSINT and licensed public-record/intel layers.
