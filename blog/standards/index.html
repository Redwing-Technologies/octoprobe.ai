Editorial Standards and Ethics

Updated Oct 17, 2026

These standards govern everything OctoProbe News publishes. They describe how we report today, and the editors enforce them before every story goes live.

Independence From the Commercial Business

OctoProbe News is published by Redwing Technologies LLC, which also sells the OctoProbe investigation platform. To keep reporting separate from that business:

  • Editors alone decide what we cover and publish. Sales, marketing and platform staff have no role in editorial decisions.
  • Platform customers, prospects, partners and data providers get no preview, approval or input on any story.
  • We do not write about a customer, prospect or partner without disclosing the relationship in the story.
  • Stories are not written to promote the platform. When our reporting uses OctoProbe tools, such as an investigation graph, the story says so.
  • No one in the newsroom works on paid client matters involving the people or organizations they cover.
  • The newsroom does not lobby, run public-relations campaigns, or represent anyone before a government body.

Sourcing and Verification

  • We work from primary records wherever they exist: filings, official documents, grant lists, archived pages and technical records.
  • Every factual claim links to its source. When a page is blocked, altered or gone, we link an archived copy and say so.
  • We label findings by strength: proven by a primary record, reported by a credible outlet, or unproven. We say plainly what we could not verify.
  • For leaked or hacked material we describe where it came from, test what we can, and state what cannot be authenticated.
  • We do not publish anonymous claims as fact. Rumors appear only when we are testing them, and we report the result.

Quotes and Attribution

  • Quotations are verbatim and link to the place they appear. We do not tidy, merge or invent quotes.
  • When we translate, we say so and keep the original-language text alongside where practical.
  • Allegations are attributed to the people who made them. A claim repeated by us is still their claim.
  • Analysis is labeled as analysis and kept separate from the factual record.

Fairness and Right of Reply

People and organizations facing significant allegations get a chance to respond. We report their public statements, and we contact them for comment where we can reach them. Responses received after publication are added and noted.

Images and Screenshots

Every screenshot is credited to its original source and linked. We crop and redact, and we say when we have. We do not otherwise alter images. Generated or illustrative art is labeled as such.

Privacy and Redaction

  • We blur or omit phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses and identity-document numbers.
  • We do not name private individuals unless they hold a public role, have been charged, or naming them is essential to the story. We never name minors.
  • People listed in leaked rosters or target databases are not named unless they are public figures, because publication can put them at risk.

Open-Source Methods

  • We use information that is public or lawfully obtained.
  • We do not log into accounts that are not ours, bypass security controls, use stolen credentials or pose as someone else to obtain information.
  • We observe websites and systems passively, as any visitor would.
  • Each investigation explains how it was reported, so readers can check our work.

Software and AI Tools

We use software, including the OctoProbe platform and AI-assisted research and drafting tools. Tools do not decide what we publish. An editor reviews every story, and every quote, figure and link is checked against its source before publication.

Bylines

Stories are published under the OctoProbe News byline because they are the product of the editorial desk, not one writer. The masthead names the roles responsible for every story.

Corrections

We fix errors promptly and openly. See our corrections policy.
