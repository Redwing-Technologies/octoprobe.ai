FAQ

Questions practitioners ask first.

What can I use as a starting identifier?
Any clue works: an email, phone number, handle, name, domain, or photo. Use one, or combine several.
What is an expansion layer?
Every identifier opens its own layer of leads. Expand a node and every source that applies runs at once. OctoProbe then points you to the most promising lead to follow next.
What sources does OctoProbe use?
OctoProbe draws on 20+ premium APIs covering breach intelligence, account discovery, phone intelligence, and digital infrastructure. It adds public records such as court filings, corporate records, and financial disclosures, plus facial recognition and web capture, all in one workspace.
How does facial recognition work?
OctoProbe finds the usable faces in your case photos and searches the open web for matches. You decide which images are searched, and every match is saved to the graph for your review.
How does OctoProbe connect the dots?
It merges duplicate results, filters out noisy identifiers, and scores each lead against your subject, so the real connections rise to the top and the noise stays out of the way.
How is evidence preserved?
Every finding is captured with its source and a timestamp, and web pages are preserved as you work. Click any link to see exactly why it's there, then export a polished report you can stand behind.
